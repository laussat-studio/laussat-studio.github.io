This Privacy Policy describes how Studio Laussat, LLC ("we") handles information when you use our products, services, and websites.

## Summary

- We aim to collect the minimum information needed to operate and improve our products.
- Some products may use standard device information (e.g. OS version) for debugging.
- If an app integrates third-party services (analytics, crash reporting, payments), the app-specific disclosures apply.

## Information we may collect

Depending on the product and how you interact with it, we may collect:

- **Support communications** you send us (email content, attachments).
- **Diagnostic information** (e.g. device model, OS version, error logs) when you report a problem.
- **Website logs** (e.g. IP address, user agent) which are typically recorded by hosting providers.

## Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and performance.

If we use analytics, we aim to configure it to minimize personal data collection.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers who help us operate our products (for example: hosting, email, crash reporting, payment processing). These providers are only permitted to use the information to provide services to us.

## Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above (for example: to resolve support issues, comply with legal obligations, or maintain security).

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date when we do.
