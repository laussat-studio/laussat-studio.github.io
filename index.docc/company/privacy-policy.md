**Effective date:** 2026-02-12

Studio Laussat, LLC (“Studio Laussat”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our websites or use our products.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- https://laussat.studio
- https://laussat-studio.github.io

Some products may provide additional, product-specific privacy disclosures in-app or on a product page.

## Information we collect

### Website access logs

When you visit our websites, our hosting providers may process standard web server logs (for example: IP address, user agent, referring URL, and timestamps). We use this information for:

- Security and abuse prevention
- Reliability and operational troubleshooting

We do not use website access logs to build profiles about you.

### Cookies and analytics

We do not intentionally use cookies for advertising, and we do not intentionally run third-party analytics trackers on our websites.

If we add analytics or other tracking in the future, we will update this policy and (where appropriate) provide choices.

### Information you provide to us

If you contact us (for example, by emailing support@laussat.studio), we will receive the information you include in your message (such as your email address and the contents of your request). We use it only to respond and provide support.

## Products

Our products may process information needed to provide their features. Where product data collection applies, we aim to:

- Collect only what is necessary to operate the product
- Limit retention to what is needed for support and operations

Product-specific privacy details (if applicable) will be shown in-app or on a product page.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate our websites or deliver support (for example, website hosting, email delivery). These providers process information on our behalf.

## Data retention

- Website access logs: retained according to our hosting providers’ standard retention periods.
- Support email: retained as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records of support interactions, and meet legal obligations.

## Your choices

- You can choose not to contact us.
- If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact us using the email below.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email: **privacy@laussat.studio**

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the “Effective date” above when we make changes.
